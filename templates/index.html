Reinforcement Learning System
How the Reinforcement Learning Works:
  1. Upload student submissions and generate automatic feedback
  2. Click "Review (RL)" to evaluate and improve the generated feedback
  3. The system collects your improvements as training data
  4. Once enough data is collected, the model can be fine-tuned
  5. The feedback quality improves over time as the model learns from your corrections
Benefits:
  • Continuous improvement in feedback quality
  • Model learns your specific feedback preferences
  • Reduces manual editing over time
  • Adapts to different assignment types
